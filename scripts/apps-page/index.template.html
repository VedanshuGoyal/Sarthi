How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. Book ahead

    Pick the trip, your car and a start time at least 45 minutes away. You see the fare before you confirm.

  2. A driver accepts

    Drivers nearby who can drive your car's gearbox get the request. You see who accepted, their rating and their number.

  3. Share the start code

    Your driver begins the trip only with the 4-digit code in your app. Follow them on the map as they come to you.

  4. Pay and rate

    When the trip ends, pay by UPI from any UPI app. Then rate each other.

Safety

You hand over the keys. We take that seriously.

Every part of a MOJUD ride is built so you know who is driving your car, when they start and where they are.

  • Documented driversDrivers give us their Aadhaar, driving licence, a selfie and bank details before their first ride.
  • Start codeThe trip can't begin without the code in your app. After five wrong tries, the code changes.
  • Live locationSee your driver on the map from an hour before the start until the trip ends.
  • Two-way ratingsCustomers rate drivers and drivers rate customers, after every trip.
  • Free to cancelChange of plans? Cancel any time before the trip starts, at no charge.
  • Told at every stepNotifications when a driver accepts, arrives, or hands the ride back.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Write to team@mojud.life.

Does MOJUD give me a car?

No. MOJUD sends a driver for your own car. Add your car once (registration, type and gearbox) and pick it when you book.

Where do you operate?

Delhi NCR: Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and nearby areas. Every pickup, stop and destination must be inside this area.

How early do I need to book?

At least 45 minutes before the start time. Drivers can accept until 30 minutes before; if nobody has by then, the booking closes as "No driver found" and nothing is charged.

How do I pay?

After the trip ends, pay the fare in the app by UPI, from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or your bank's UPI app. Payments go through PhonePe's payment gateway; MOJUD never sees your UPI PIN.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge, any time before the trip starts. See the refund and cancellation policy.

My car is automatic. Will the driver know how to drive it?

Yes. Drivers tell us whether they drive manual, automatic or both, and only drivers who can drive your car's gearbox are offered your ride.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. The apps are on Android first; iPhone comes after Google Play.
