In short

  • MOJUD connects you with an independent, verified driver who drives your own car.
  • Book at least 45 minutes ahead. Cancelling is free until the trip starts.
  • Pay by UPI in the app after the trip. While a trip is unpaid, you can't book again.
  • Your car's insurance and condition are your responsibility.
  • You can delete your account in the app.

This summary helps you find your way. The full terms below are what apply.

1Who we are

They apply when you use the MOJUD app, the MOJUD Driver app, or this website. By creating an account, you agree to them. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

2What MOJUD is

  • MOJUD is a platform. It connects customers who need someone to drive their car with independent drivers.
  • We don't provide cars. The driver drives the customer's own car.
  • Drivers are independent. They are not MOJUD's employees or agents. They choose when to go on duty and which rides to accept.
  • We operate in Delhi NCR.
  • You can book three kinds of trip:
    • Round Trip: a driver for a number of hours.
    • One-Way: from your pickup to one destination.
    • Multi-Stop: several stops in one trip.
  • Before a driver can take rides, we check their identity through DigiLocker, their driving licence and their bank account, and our team reviews their selfie. These checks lower the risk, but they can't guarantee how anyone will behave.

3Your account

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code we send by SMS.
  • Give true, current details, and keep them up to date.
  • Keep your phone secure. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One account per person. Don't share, sell or transfer it. One identity can have only one driver account.
  • Install the apps only from apps.mojud.life.

4Booking a driver

  • Choose the trip, your car and a start time at least 45 minutes from now.
  • You see the fare before you book.
  • A booking is a request. It is confirmed when a driver accepts it. The app then shows you the driver's name, photo, rating and phone number.
  • If no driver has accepted it 30 minutes before the start time, the booking closes, the app shows "No driver found", and you pay nothing.
  • A driver may hand back a ride before it starts. We'll tell you and offer the ride to other drivers, but we can't promise another driver will take it.
  • The trip starts only when the driver enters the 4-digit start code shown in your app. Give the code only to the driver, in person.
  • If a trip runs longer than booked: [[EXTRA TIME POLICY: what happens and what it costs]]

5Cancellation and no-show

Customers

  • You can cancel in the app at any time before the trip starts. It's free until your driver reaches the pickup.
  • Once your driver is at the pickup, cancelling costs ₹50. The app shows this before you confirm. The fee goes to the driver, less MOJUD's commission.
  • Once a trip has started, you can end it early in the app. You pay the hourly rate for the time used: each started hour counts as a full hour, plus any late-night charge for those hours. You never pay more than the fare you booked.
  • If you're not at the pickup, the driver waits 15 minutes from when they arrived or from your booked start time, whichever is later. After that they can close the ride. There's no fee for a no-show.

Drivers

  • If you can't make a ride you accepted, hand it back in the app as early as you can, so another driver can take it.
  • [[DRIVER LATE HAND-BACK AND NO-SHOW POLICY: consequences, if any]]

6Fares and payment

  • Fares are in Indian rupees and include all taxes, including GST.
  • You pay after the trip ends, by UPI in the app, through PhonePe. A payment counts only once PhonePe confirms it to us.
  • PhonePe handles the payment, and its terms also apply. We never see your UPI PIN.
  • MOJUD collects the fare and pays the driver their share (see section 11).
  • Fuel, tolls, parking and other costs of running your car on the trip are yours. [[RUNNING COSTS: confirm, and how a driver who pays one for you is repaid]]
  • If you are charged twice, or charged by mistake, contact us and we'll refund the extra amount. [[REFUND TIMELINE]]

7If a trip isn't paid

  • Until you pay for a completed trip, you can't make new bookings.
  • You also can't delete your account while a completed trip is unpaid.
  • We may contact you to collect what you owe. [[FURTHER STEPS FOR UNPAID TRIPS, e.g. late fee or recovery, if any]]

8Customer responsibilities

  • Your car must be legal to drive. That means valid registration, insurance as the law requires, a valid pollution certificate and any permit it needs. Keep the papers in the car as the law requires.
  • Your car must be safe. Brakes, tyres, lights and the rest must be in working order. The car's insurance and condition are your responsibility, not MOJUD's or the driver's.
  • Give the right pickup, stops and car details, including the gearbox, so we send a driver who can drive it.
  • Be at the pickup at the start time.
  • Don't ask the driver to do anything illegal or unsafe. For example: speeding, breaking traffic rules, carrying illegal goods, or carrying more people than the car has seats.
  • Treat the driver with respect. Abuse, harassment or violence will get your account closed.
  • You are responsible for your passengers and for anything you leave in the car.

9Accidents, damage and fines

MOJUD does not pay for damage to your car, including damage during a trip. [[DAMAGE AND LIABILITY POLICY: who pays for traffic fines (challans) and for third-party claims arising during a trip; how and by when to report it]]

Report any accident, damage or traffic fine from a trip to us as soon as you can at [[CONTACT EMAIL]], with the booking ID. In an emergency, call 112 first.

10Driver responsibilities

  • Be 18 or older and hold a valid driving licence for the kind of car you drive. Tell us at once if your licence expires or is suspended or cancelled.
  • Complete verification honestly: Aadhaar through DigiLocker, your driving licence, your bank account and a selfie. Only verified drivers get rides. We may ask you to verify again.
  • Accept only rides you can do. Be on time. Drive only cars with a gearbox you told us you can drive.
  • Drive safely and within the law. Never drive after alcohol or drugs, or when you are too tired. Don't use a handheld phone while driving.
  • Start a trip only with the customer's start code. End it in the app when the trip ends.
  • Keep location sharing on while you are on duty. The app uses it to show you ride requests and to let your customer track the trip.
  • Look after the customer's car and belongings. Don't use the car for anything outside the trip.
  • Use the customer's phone number, address and trip details only for that ride. Don't keep, share or use them for anything else.
  • Don't take payment for a MOJUD ride outside the app.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes on what you earn. [[TAX: any tax MOJUD must deduct or report on driver earnings]]

11Driver earnings and payouts

  • For each completed ride, MOJUD keeps a commission of 13% of the fare. The rest is yours. The same applies to a cancellation fee a customer pays after you reached the pickup.
  • Your share becomes due once the customer has paid. If a customer doesn't pay, MOJUD still pays you your share once it closes that trip's bill.
  • We pay by bank transfer to the bank account you verified in the app. [[PAYOUT SCHEDULE]]
  • We can't pay you until your verification and bank account are confirmed. Until then, what you've earned stays owed to you.
  • The Earnings screen shows what customers still owe, what we owe you, and what we have paid.

12Ratings

  • After a completed trip, the customer and the driver can each rate the other once, from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional note.
  • Your average rating is shown to the other side on later rides.
  • Rate honestly. Don't use the note to abuse anyone or to share personal details.
  • We may use ratings and notes to keep the service safe, including when we decide on suspensions.

13Safety

  • In an emergency, call 112. The customer app has a button for it under Help & safety.
  • Under Help & safety, customers can also share trip details on WhatsApp with someone they choose, and contact MOJUD support.
  • Tell us about any safety problem, during or after a trip.

14Suspension and closing accounts

We may suspend or close an account, or take a driver off the road, if:

  • you break these terms or the law;
  • you give false details or commit fraud;
  • there are safety complaints or serious misconduct;
  • there are repeated low ratings, no-shows or late cancellations [[THRESHOLDS, if any]]; or
  • a driver's verification fails, expires or is withdrawn.

When a driver is taken off the road, rides they accepted but haven't started go back to other drivers, and the customers are told. A trip already under way can finish.

Earnings already due to a driver are still paid, except [[CASES WHERE EARNINGS CAN BE WITHHELD, e.g. fraud]].

If you think we got it wrong, contact our Grievance Officer (section 19).

15Deleting your account

  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: open the Account tab and tap Delete account. This works the same in MOJUD and MOJUD Driver. You can also ask us by email.
  • We can't delete it while a driver has accepted a ride, has arrived, or the trip is under way. We also can't delete it while a completed trip is unpaid.
  • When you delete it, we remove your personal details and sign you out on every device.
  • Customers: bookings no driver has taken yet are cancelled.
  • Drivers: earnings not yet paid out are still owed to you. Contact us before you delete, so we can pay them.
  • We keep records of rides and payments, without your name, phone number or email. The Privacy Policy explains what we keep and why.
  • Deletion can't be undone. You can sign up again later with the same number.
