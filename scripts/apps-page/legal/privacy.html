In short

  • We collect what we need to run trips, take payments, pay drivers and check drivers' identity.
  • We never store a driver's full Aadhaar number.
  • A driver's live location is kept only in our server's memory, not in our database.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account in the app. Ride and payment records are kept, without your name, phone number or email.

This summary helps you find your way. The full policy below is what applies.

1Who we are

[[REGISTERED ENTITY NAME AND ADDRESS]] ("MOJUD", "we", "us") runs the MOJUD app for customers, the MOJUD Driver app for drivers, and this website. We decide how your personal data is used, so we are responsible for it.

We use your data with the consent you give when you sign up. When you sign up, we record which version of this policy you agreed to, and when. Where the law allows, we also use data without consent, for example to meet a legal duty.

2What we collect from customers

In the MOJUD app:

  • Account: your name, mobile number, email and date of birth. We use your date of birth to check that you are 18 or older.
  • Cars: the registration number, type and gearbox of each car you save.
  • Trips: pickup, stops and destination (addresses and map points), date and time, the car, the fare, the start code, the trip's status, any reason you give for cancelling, and the ratings and notes you give and get.
  • Recent pickups: a short list of places you were picked up from, so booking is quicker.
  • Location: if you allow it, your phone's location while you use the app, to fill in your pickup and to look up addresses. The app doesn't use your location in the background.
  • Payments: PhonePe's order IDs, the UPI transaction reference (UTR), the payment method and whether it succeeded. We never see your UPI PIN.
  • Notifications: a token from your phone that lets us send you trip updates.
  • Support: what you tell us when you contact us.

3What we collect from drivers

In the MOJUD Driver app:

  • Account: your name, mobile number, date of birth, gender, and the gearbox you can drive.
  • Aadhaar, through DigiLocker: when you approve it in DigiLocker, we receive the verified name, date of birth and the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar. We never store your full Aadhaar number. We also keep a one-way, keyed fingerprint of those verified details, so one identity can have only one driver account.
  • Driving licence: the number, name, expiry date and vehicle classes, from a licence check.
  • Bank account: the account holder's name, the account number (stored encrypted), its last 4 digits, the IFSC, and the name the bank confirmed through a penny-drop check (a tiny test transfer).
  • Selfie: a photo of you, taken in the app with the camera. Our team reviews it, and customers see a small copy once you accept their ride. Older versions of the app also uploaded photos of the Aadhaar card and driving licence. We keep those on the same terms as the selfie.
  • Verification: your verification status, our reviewers' notes, and a log of each check we ran (the kind of check, the result, and details such as the licence number or the last 4 digits of the Aadhaar or bank account).
  • Location: while you are on duty, the app shares your location about every 10 seconds, also when it is in the background. We use it to show you nearby ride requests, to match rides and to let your customer track the trip. It is held only in our server's memory and is not saved in our database.
  • Rides and money: the rides you accepted, drove or swiped away, your earnings, payouts and bank transfer references, and the ratings you give and get.
  • Notifications: a token from your phone that lets us send you ride requests and updates.

4Logs and your phone

  • Web server logs record IP addresses, times and the address of each request. Some requests carry map points, so those appear in the logs too.
  • App logs identify accounts by an ID, not by name or phone number.
  • Sign-in codes are sent by our SMS provider. We don't store the codes, only a short-lived count of attempts, in memory.
  • On your phone, the apps keep your sign-in token. The customer app also keeps your recent pickups, and the driver app keeps the verification photos you took. Both apps clear these when you sign out.

5What the other side sees

  • Before a driver accepts, nearby drivers see the trip details, but not your name or phone number.
  • Once a driver accepts, the customer sees the driver's name, selfie, rating and phone number. The driver sees the customer's name, phone number, rating, the pickup and stops, and the car.
  • Live tracking: the customer sees the driver on the map from 60 minutes before the start, and during the trip.
  • The start code is shown only to the customer, while the driver is on the way or at the pickup. It is never put in a notification.
  • Share trip: if you use it, you choose who gets the message on WhatsApp.

6Why we use your data

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To match bookings with nearby drivers who can drive your car.
  • To run the trip: tracking, the start code and notifications.
  • To take payments, pay drivers and handle refunds.
  • To check that drivers are who they say they are, are 18 or older, hold a valid licence and own the bank account we pay.
  • To keep the service safe: ratings, support, and dealing with complaints and incidents.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, for example one driver account per identity, and limits on sign-in codes.
  • To keep accounts and records, and to meet our legal and tax duties.
  • To understand how the service is used, from counts and totals.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

9Deleting your account

Open the Account tab and tap Delete account. This works the same in both apps. You can also ask us by email (section 14).

We can't delete an account while a driver has accepted a ride, has arrived, or the trip is under way. We also can't delete it while a completed trip is unpaid.

We don't remove the account row itself, because ride and payment records point to it. Instead, we anonymise it.

What we remove, for everyone

  • Your name, phone number, email and date of birth are removed or replaced, so the account no longer identifies you.
  • You are signed out on every device at once.
  • Your notification tokens are deleted.
  • You can sign up again later with the same number.

Customers

  • Your saved cars and recent pickups are deleted.
  • Bookings that no driver has taken are cancelled.

Drivers

  • Your bank account, the rides you swiped away, and all verification details (DigiLocker, licence, selfie and review notes) are deleted.
  • Your photos are deleted from storage, and you are taken off duty.
  • The licence number and Aadhaar digits are removed from the log of verification checks. The rest of that log is deleted after 180 days.
  • Earnings not yet paid out are still owed to you. Contact us so we can pay them.

What we keep

  • Records of rides and payments: bookings, trip details (such as pickup and stop addresses, and the car), fares, payments, payouts and ratings. We keep them for accounting, tax and legal reasons, and to deal with disputes. They are no longer linked to your name, phone number or email.
  • If our team rejected a driver's verification, we keep the fingerprint of that driver's verified Aadhaar details, so the same identity can't come back on a new account.

10Your rights and choices

You can do the following. These follow the rights in India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

  • Access your data: ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it, and who we've shared it with.
  • Correct it: drivers can update their verification details in the app. For anything else, contact us.
  • Erase it: delete your account in the app, or ask us (see section 9 for what we must keep).
  • Withdraw consent: delete your account. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself.
  • Complain: contact our Grievance Officer. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use a right, email us from, or tell us, the mobile number on your account. We may need to confirm it's you before we act. We aim to reply within [[GRIEVANCE RESPONSE TIME]].

You can also turn off location or notifications in your phone's settings. Features that need them won't work, and drivers can't take rides without location.

11How we protect it

  • The apps talk to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • You sign in with a one-time code. We keep only a fingerprint of your sign-in token, not the token itself.
  • Bank account numbers are stored encrypted. We never store a full Aadhaar number.
  • Drivers' photos are kept in private storage. Only the driver and our reviewers can open them. A customer sees only a small copy of their own driver's selfie.
  • Only authorised people can use our admin tools.
  • Our app logs avoid names and phone numbers.
  • We back up our database every day.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12Children

  • MOJUD is only for people aged 18 or older. We check a customer's age at sign-up, and a driver's age during verification.
  • We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

  • If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it applies, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
